Acme Corp Shipping FAQ

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
Orders ship within 24 hours on working days.
Customers get a tracking number by email once the order ships.
If a package is lost, contact the Logistics department. A replacement is sent after 10 business days without delivery.
We ship to the US, Canada, and the UK only.
