Acme Corp Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the duplicate charge is refunded in full within 5 to 7 business days. The agent must first confirm two identical transactions in the billing system.
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We do not refund to a different card or account.
Digital products are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.
Refunds above 500 dollars need approval from the Accounting department.
